## Key Concepts Tested
- AWS Well-Architected Framework (6 pillars)
- Multi-tier architecture design
- Decoupled, event-driven patterns
- High availability & fault tolerance
- Security best practices (least privilege, defense in depth)
- Cost optimization strategies
- Data protection (encryption at rest & in transit)
- Disaster recovery strategies

## SAA-C03 Question Lens
- The exam is mainly scenario-based. The correct answer usually balances security, resilience, performance, cost, and operational overhead.
- "Most secure" usually points to least privilege, private connectivity, encryption, managed rotation, and centralized governance.
- "Highly available" usually means multiple Availability Zones first; use multiple Regions when the question mentions regional disasters, global users, or strict DR goals.
- "High-performing" usually means selecting the right purpose-built service, caching where safe, scaling horizontally, and avoiding bottlenecks such as single instances or hot partitions.
- "Cost-optimized" usually means matching the purchasing model and storage tier to access patterns, avoiding unnecessary cross-AZ/cross-Region transfer, and using managed/serverless services when utilization is variable.

## Exam Tips
- No penalty for guessing — answer every question
- Questions are scenario-based — focus on "most appropriate" solution
- Look for keywords: "most cost-effective", "least operational overhead", "highest availability"
- Compensatory scoring — you don't need to pass every domain individually
- Time management: ~1.6 minutes per question
